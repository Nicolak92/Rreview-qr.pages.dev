How to Build UTM Links to Track Where Your Visitors Come From

If you share the same link in many places, you cannot tell which one brings visitors. UTM tags fix that by adding a short label to each link.

What the parts mean

  • utm_source: where the visit comes from, such as facebook or newsletter.
  • utm_medium: the type of channel, such as social, email or cpc.
  • utm_campaign: the name of the campaign or promotion.
  • utm_term and utm_content: optional. Used for paid keywords and to tell ads apart.

How to build a link

  1. Open the UTM Link Builder and enter the page you want people to visit.
  2. Name your campaign, for example spring_sale.
  3. Choose the channels where you will share the link. Each one gets its own link.
  4. Copy the links and use them in your posts, emails and ads.

Good habits

Use lowercase and the same names every time, so your reports stay clean. Analytics tools treat Facebook and facebook as two different sources. Do not use UTM links for links inside your own website, because they can break your analytics sessions.

Where you see the results

UTM tags are read by Google Analytics and most other analytics tools. They only work if your site uses one of them.
