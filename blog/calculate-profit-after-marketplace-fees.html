How to Calculate Your Profit After Marketplace Fees

A sale is not the same as profit. After the marketplace fee, the payment fee, shipping and packaging, you may keep much less than you think. Here is how to find your real numbers.

What fees to include

  • Marketplace or transaction fee (a percentage)
  • Payment processing fee (a percentage plus a fixed amount)
  • Listing fees and advertising
  • Shipping, packaging and returns

How to calculate it

  1. Open the Marketplace Fee and Profit Calculator and enter your selling price, shipping and what the item costs you.
  2. Enter your marketplace and payment fees, or start from an example and edit it.
  3. Read your profit per sale, margin and markup.
  4. Check the break-even price and the price you need for your target margin.

Margin and markup are not the same

Margin is your profit divided by the money you receive. Markup is your profit divided by your cost. A 50% markup is only a 33% margin. Mixing them up is a common way to underprice your products.

Use your own fee rates

The calculator’s examples are only starting points. Fees change often and differ by country, category, seller plan and payment method. Check your marketplace’s current fee page and type in your real numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work for Etsy, eBay, Amazon and Shopify?

Yes. It works for any marketplace. Enter the fee rates for the one you use, or start from an example and edit it.

What is the break-even price?

It is the lowest selling price at which you make zero profit after all fees and costs.

Are the example fees up to date?

They are only examples. Marketplace fees change, so always check the current rates on the marketplace’s own fee page.
