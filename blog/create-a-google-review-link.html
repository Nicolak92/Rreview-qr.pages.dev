How to Create a Google Review Link and Share It

A Google review link takes people straight to your review form, so they do not have to search for you first. You can send it in a message, an email or on a receipt.

Why a direct link helps

If you only say “please review us on Google,” people have to search for your business, find the right listing and look for the review button. A direct link opens your review form in one tap.

How to create one

  1. Find your business on Google Maps.
  2. Use Share and copy the link.
  3. Paste it into the Google Review Link Generator.
  4. Copy the new link and open it on your phone to test it.

Where to share it

  • In a text message or email after a job or visit
  • On invoices and receipts
  • In your email signature
  • On your website and social media profiles

A simple message to copy

“Thanks for choosing us today! If you have a minute, we would really appreciate a quick Google review: [your link]”

Keep it short, send it soon after the visit, and thank people whether or not they reply. Do not offer rewards for reviews, because Google’s rules do not allow it.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use a link or a QR code?

Use the link in messages, emails and online. Use a QR code on anything printed. You can make both for free.

Is the link generator free?

Yes. It is free and needs no sign-up.

Do customers need a Google account?

Yes. To leave a review, customers need to be signed in to Google. If they are not, Google asks them to sign in first.
