How to Find a Good Username (Free Generator)

Your username is how people find and remember you. A short, clear one is easier to share, type and say out loud.

Tips for a good username

  • Keep it short and easy to say out loud
  • Avoid too many numbers and symbols
  • Use the same name on every platform if you can
  • Do not use private details such as your birth date or address

How to find ideas

  1. Open the Username Generator and type your name or a word you like.
  2. Add an interest to make it more personal. This is optional.
  3. Pick a style and the platform you will use.
  4. Press Generate usernames, star your favorites and check them on the platform.

Why you pick a platform

Each platform limits length and characters. When you choose a platform, the tool removes characters it does not allow and keeps names within the limit. Rules can change, so check the platform when you sign up.

Check it before you commit

The generator cannot tell you if a name is free. Popular names are often taken, so try your favorites on the platform and keep a few spare.

Frequently asked questions

Does it check if a username is taken?

No. Try the name on the platform. Popular names are often taken, so keep a few favorites ready.

Is my name stored?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved.

Which platforms are supported?

Instagram, TikTok, X, Twitch, YouTube and a general option that works almost everywhere.
