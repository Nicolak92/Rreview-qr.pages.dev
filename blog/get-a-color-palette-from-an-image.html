How to Get a Color Palette From an Image or Logo

Found a photo or logo with colors you love? You can pull its main colors out in seconds and use them in your website, slides or posters.

What it is good for

  • Matching your website colors to your logo
  • Building a brand palette from a product photo
  • Finding colors for slides, posters and social posts
  • Getting a design idea from a photo you like

How to do it

  1. Open the Color Palette from Image tool and choose a photo, logo or screenshot, or drop it on the box.
  2. Pick how many colors you want. The main colors appear below the image.
  3. Click the image to add any exact color you want, such as a small detail.
  4. Click a color to copy its HEX code, or copy the whole palette as CSS or JSON.

Tips for better results

  • Choose a sharp, well-lit image. A photo with one clear subject usually gives a cleaner palette.
  • A PNG or JPG logo works well. Transparent areas are ignored.
  • Keep 3 to 5 colors. A small palette is easier to use everywhere.

When you have your colors, check that your text and background are easy to read together with the Color Contrast Checker.

Your image stays private

The image is read in your browser. It is not uploaded or saved anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser, so your image never leaves your device.

What can I copy?

HEX codes, CSS variables and JSON. You can also download the palette as a PNG image.

Can I use a logo?

Yes. A PNG or JPG logo works well. Transparent areas are ignored.
