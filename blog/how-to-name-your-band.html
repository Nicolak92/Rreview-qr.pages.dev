How to Find a Great Band Name (Free Generator)

A band name goes on posters, playlists and shirts, so it has to look good and be easy to remember. Here is a simple way to find one.

What makes a good band name

  • Easy to say, spell and remember
  • Fits your sound and your look
  • Looks good on a poster or a playlist cover

How to find ideas

  1. Open the Band Name Generator and choose the genre closest to your sound, such as rock, metal, pop, indie, hip-hop, electronic, jazz, country or punk.
  2. Add a word you like, such as a place, an animal or a mood. This is optional.
  3. Press Generate names until something fits, and star your favorites.
  4. Use the search link beside a name to see whether it is already used.

Say it and see it

Say the name out loud, then picture it on a poster. A name that sounds great but is hard to read, or the other way round, will cost you fans. Try it in capital letters and in lowercase.

Check before you commit

Many bands share similar names. Before you print shirts, search streaming apps, social media and domain names. Avoid names that are close to a well-known act.
