How to Name Your Podcast (With a Free Name Generator)

Your podcast name is the first thing people see in a podcast app, so it has to be clear, easy to say and easy to remember. Here is how to find one without getting stuck.

What makes a good podcast name

  • Easy to say and to spell
  • Tells people what the show is about
  • Short enough to read in a podcast app. Two to four words works well.
  • Not too close to a well-known show

How to find ideas

  1. Open the Podcast Name Generator and type your topic in one to three words.
  2. Choose a style: catchy, professional, funny or short.
  3. Press Generate names until something sparks, and star the ones you like.
  4. Use the search link beside a name to see whether it is already taken.

Check before you commit

The generator cannot tell you if a name is free. Before you decide, search for it on Apple Podcasts and Spotify, on social media and as a web address. Also make sure the name does not copy someone else’s trademark.

Say it out loud

Say the name aloud and ask two or three friends to spell it after hearing it once. If they struggle, listeners will too. Pick a name you can see yourself saying at the start of every episode.
