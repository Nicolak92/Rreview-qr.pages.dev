How to Make a Google Review Sign for Your Business

A good review sign does two jobs: it tells customers what to do, and it makes doing it quick. Here is how to make one that gets noticed.

What a good review sign has

  • A short headline, like “Enjoyed your visit?”
  • One line that says what to do: “Scan to leave a Google review.”
  • A QR code that is large, with empty space around it
  • Your logo and colors, so it looks like part of your business

How to make one

  1. Open the Review Sign Maker.
  2. Add your Google Maps link.
  3. Choose a message, a color design and a size.
  4. Add your logo if you like.
  5. Download the PNG and print it.

Poster, counter card or sticker?

A poster suits a wall or window. A counter card sits by the till or on a table. A sticker fits packaging, cards and other small spaces. Many businesses use two: a card where people pay and a sticker on takeaway bags.

Printing tips

  • Print at full size. Do not shrink the QR code to fit a small space.
  • Keep strong contrast between the code and its background.
  • Avoid glossy paper in bright light, because glare makes scanning harder.
  • Scan the printed sign with your phone before you print more.
