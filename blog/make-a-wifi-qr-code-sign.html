How to Make a Wi-Fi QR Code Sign for Your Guests

Guests ask for the Wi-Fi password all the time. A sign with a QR code answers the question for you: they scan it and their phone offers to join the network.

How a Wi-Fi QR code works

The code holds your network name and password. When a guest scans it with their phone camera, the phone offers to join the network. They do not have to type anything.

How to make the sign

  1. Type your network name and password exactly as they appear on your router.
  2. Choose the security type. Most networks use WPA / WPA2 / WPA3.
  3. Pick a design and a size, and add your logo if you like.
  4. Download the PNG, print it, and scan it once with your own phone to test.

Where to put it

  • Cafes, restaurants and shops
  • Hotel rooms, guest houses and holiday rentals
  • Offices, waiting rooms and meeting rooms
  • Your home, for visitors

Keep it safe

Use a separate guest network, not your main one. If the password gets shared too widely, change it and print a new sign. Never print the password to your router’s admin page.

Frequently asked questions

Is my Wi-Fi password sent anywhere?

No. The sign is made in your browser. Your network name, password and logo are not uploaded or stored.

Which phones can scan it?

Recent iPhones and Android phones can scan it with the normal camera app. Some older Android phones need a QR scanner app or Google Lens.

What if I change my password?

The old sign will stop working. Make a new one with the new password.
