How to Make a Professional Email Signature (Free)

Your email signature goes on every message you send. A clean one tells people who you are and how to reach you, and it makes you look professional.

How to make one

  1. Open the Email Signature Generator and fill in your details. Leave out anything you do not want.
  2. Pick a layout, a font and an accent color.
  3. Press Copy signature and paste it into your email settings, or use Copy HTML code.

How to add it to your email

  • Gmail: Settings, See all settings, Signature. Paste the copied signature.
  • Outlook: Settings, Accounts, Signatures. Paste the copied signature.
  • Apple Mail: Settings, Signatures. Paste it, and turn off “Always match my default message font”.

Tips for a good signature

Keep it short. Use one logo or photo that is already hosted on the web, and use text for your contact details so it still works when images are turned off.

About the logo

Email programs show images from a web address and often block images embedded in the signature itself. Upload your logo to your website or an image host, then paste its address into the tool.
