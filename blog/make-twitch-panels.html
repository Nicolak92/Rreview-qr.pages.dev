How to Make Twitch Panels for Your Channel (Free)

Panels sit under your stream and tell new viewers who you are, when you stream and how to support you. Matching panels make a channel look tidy and trusted.

Which panels to have

  • About me and what you stream
  • Schedule
  • Chat rules
  • Social links and Discord
  • Donations, subs and support

How to make them

  1. Open the Twitch Panel Maker and write one panel per line. Use a bar to add small text, like “About Me | Who I am”.
  2. Pick a design, a height and a border style. Add an emoji icon if you like.
  3. Download each panel, or press Download all.
  4. On Twitch, open your channel, turn on Edit Panels, add an image panel and upload the file.

What size should panels be?

Twitch panels are 320 pixels wide. The height is up to you. Choose the 2x size for sharper images on large screens.

Design tips

Keep the same colors and fonts on every panel so your channel looks tidy. Use short titles, because panels are small. Add your links on Twitch when you upload each panel.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Twitch panel maker free?

Yes. Make and download as many panels as you like, with no sign-up.

Can I add links?

Add the link on Twitch when you upload the panel. In Twitch’s panel editor, you can set a link for each image panel.

Are my panels uploaded anywhere?

No. They are made in your browser and never leave your device.
