How to Resize One Image for Every Social Media Size (Free)

Each social network wants a different image size. Instead of editing the same picture again and again, you can make every size from one image in a few clicks.

How to do it

  1. Open the Social Media Image Resizer and choose your image (JPG, PNG or WebP).
  2. Tick every size you need, such as an Instagram post and a YouTube thumbnail.
  3. Choose Fill and crop or Fit inside, and move the image to choose what is kept.
  4. Download one size, or all the sizes you ticked.

Fill and crop, or fit inside?

  • Fill and crop: the image covers the whole area and the edges may be cut off.
  • Fit inside: the whole image is shown, with a background color around it.

Get the best quality

Start with a large image. If your picture is smaller than the size you choose, it is enlarged and may look soft. Use JPG for photos, because the files are smaller. Use PNG for graphics and text, because it keeps sharp edges.

Check the current sizes

Platforms change their recommended sizes from time to time. The sizes in the tool are common choices, so check the platform’s help page for its latest guidance.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser, so your image never leaves your device.

Can I make several sizes at once?

Yes. Tick all the sizes you need and press Download all selected.

Will it make my image blurry?
