How to Write a Meta Description That Gets Clicks

The page title and description are what people see in Google before they click. Good ones can bring more visitors to the same page.

What they are

The meta title is the headline of your result in search. The meta description is the short text under it. You add both to the head of your page.

How long they should be

  • Title: about 50 to 60 characters
  • Description: about 120 to 155 characters
  • Put the important words first, because the end may be cut off.

How to write one

  1. Open the Meta Description Generator and enter your main keyword, what the page offers and its main benefit.
  2. Press Suggest titles and descriptions, then click Use beside the ones you like.
  3. Edit the text and watch the length meters and the search preview.
  4. Copy the HTML and paste it into the head of your page.

What makes a good description

Describe the page honestly, include the main keyword once, and give a reason to click. Give every page its own description, not a copy of another page.

Frequently asked questions

Does a meta description help rankings?

It is not a direct ranking factor, but a clear description can get more people to click your result.

Will Google always use mine?

No. Google sometimes writes its own snippet from the page. A good description is used more often.
