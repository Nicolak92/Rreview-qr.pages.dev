About Toolzweb

Toolzweb is a growing collection of free online tools for small businesses, designers, online sellers, creators and freelancers. Every tool is designed to be quick, simple and useful without an account.

How the tools work

Most tools run entirely in your browser. When you upload a logo or an image, it stays on your device. A few tools, such as the Google review QR code generator, send the link you paste to our server only to expand shortened Google Maps links. We do not store it.

How we pay for it

The tools are free. We may collect email addresses from visitors who choose to subscribe, and some pages contain affiliate links to products we think are useful. See our affiliate disclosure.

Who makes it

Toolzweb is built and maintained by the author of The Ultimate Guide, a blog about digital skills. Suggestions for new tools are welcome. Contact us through the contact options on our blog.
