How to Get More Google Reviews With a QR Code

Most happy customers never leave a review, simply because it takes too many steps. A QR code removes the steps: they scan it, and your review form opens.

Why a QR code works

People are most willing to review right after a good experience, often while they are still in your shop or finishing a meal. A QR code lets them do it on the spot with their phone camera. They do not have to search for your business or type anything.

How to make one

  1. Open your business on Google Maps and copy its share link.
  2. Paste the link into the Google Review QR Code Generator.
  3. Download the QR code.
  4. Scan it with your own phone to check that it opens your review form.

Where to put it

  • Next to the till or on the front desk
  • On table cards, menus and packaging
  • On receipts, invoices and business cards
  • On a window or door sticker

Add a short line next to the code, such as “Enjoyed your visit? Scan to leave a review.” People scan more when they know what will happen.

Ask the right way

Ask every customer, not only the ones you expect to be happy. Google’s rules do not allow offering rewards in return for reviews, so keep it a simple, friendly request. Reply to the reviews you get. It shows new customers that you pay attention.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Google review QR code generator free?

Yes. It is free and needs no sign-up.

Does the QR code expire?

No. It is a normal QR code with no expiry date. If you change or move your business listing, scan the code again to make sure it still opens your review form.

How big should I print it?

Big enough to scan comfortably from about an arm’s length away. Always test one printed copy before you print many.
