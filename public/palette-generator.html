How to use the color palette generator

  1. Press Generate or the Space bar for five new colors. Use Lock to keep the ones you like.
  2. Pick a harmony, a base color, or extract colors from an image.
  3. Or browse the trending palettes below the generator and click one.
  4. In the preview, copy a single HEX code, copy the whole palette as HEX, CSS or JSON, or get more similar palettes.

Color harmonies explained

  • Analogous: neighboring colors on the wheel. Calm and cohesive.
  • Complementary: opposite colors. High contrast and energetic.
  • Triadic: three evenly spaced colors. Lively but balanced.
  • Monochrome: one hue in different lightness levels. Clean and safe.

Frequently asked questions

Is the palette generator free?

Yes. Generate and export as many palettes as you like, with no sign-up.

How do I get more palettes like one I like?

Click any palette in the gallery, then use the "More like this" section. Click one of the results to see it larger, and ask for more.

How do I keep one color and change the others?

Press Lock under the color you want to keep in the generator, then generate again.

What do the trending palettes are based on?

They are inspired by public color forecasts and paint-brand picks, such as olive greens, luminous blues, plum browns and soft whites. They are interpretations, not official color codes.

What does the contrast score mean?

It shows how readable black or white text is on that color. A ratio of 4.5 or higher (AA) is the usual target for normal text.

Does it store my images or colors?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Images are never uploaded.
