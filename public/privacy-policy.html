Privacy Policy

How Toolzweb handles your data.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Toolzweb (https://toolzweb.com) offers free online tools. You can contact us through the contact options on our blog.

What stays in your browser

Most tools run in your browser. Colors, text, images and logos you use in a tool are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

What our server processes

When you paste a shortened Google Maps link into the Google review tools, the link is sent to our server (hosted on Cloudflare) so we can expand it. We do not store these links or build profiles from them. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs.

Email signups

If you subscribe, we collect your email address through our email provider, Kit. We use it to send the free tools and tips you asked for. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. See Kit's privacy policy for how they process data.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count visits and see which tools are used. We do not use these analytics to identify you personally.

Fonts and third parties

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is shared with Google when a page loads. Some tools load open-source code libraries from a content delivery network. Links to other websites, including affiliate partners, are governed by those sites' own policies and may set their own cookies.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, such as your email address. Contact us through the contact options on our blog and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
