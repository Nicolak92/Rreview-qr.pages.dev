How to use your review sign

  1. Paste your Google Maps link and press Load.
  2. Pick a design, a size and add your logo.
  3. Download the PNG.
  4. Print it (A4 poster, 6x4 inch card, square sign or round sticker) and scan it once to test.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Make and download as many signs as you like.

Does it work with short Google Maps links?

Yes. Paste a maps.app.goo.gl link or a full Google Maps link.

What file do I get?

A high-resolution PNG. Print the poster on A4 paper at 100% scale for sharp results.

Where should I put it?

Near the counter, at the table, on the door, or on packaging. Place it where customers are already finished and happy.
