How to find a band name

  1. Choose the genre closest to your sound.
  2. Add a word you like, such as a place, an animal or a mood. This is optional.
  3. Press Generate names until something fits. Star your favorites.
  4. Search each favorite to check it is not already used.

Tips for a great band name

  • Make it easy to say, spell and remember
  • Make sure it fits your sound and your look
  • Say it out loud, and check how it looks on a poster
  • Check streaming apps, social media and domain names

Check before you commit

Many bands share similar names. Search before you print shirts, and avoid names that are close to a well-known act.

Is the band name generator free?

Yes. Generate as many names as you like, with no sign-up.

Does it use AI?

No. It mixes word lists and name patterns for each genre, so nothing is sent or stored.

Are the names available?

The tool cannot check. Use the search link beside each name, then check streaming apps and social media.

Can I add my own word?

Yes. Type a word in the box and it will be used in many of the names.

Can I use the names for a fictional band?

Yes. They work for real bands, game characters, stories and school projects.
