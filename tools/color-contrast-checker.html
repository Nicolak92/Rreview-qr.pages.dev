How to check color contrast

  1. Enter your text color and your background color, or pick them with the color boxes.
  2. Read the contrast ratio. Higher is easier to read.
  3. Check the pass or fail marks for each WCAG level.
  4. If it fails, use the suggested colors that are closest to yours.

What the levels mean

  • AA normal text: 4.5 to 1 or higher. The usual target.
  • AA large text: 3 to 1 or higher. Large means about 24 px, or 19 px bold.
  • AAA: 7 to 1 for normal text and 4.5 to 1 for large text. The strictest level.
  • UI parts: 3 to 1 for icons, borders and form fields.

Why it matters

Low contrast is hard to read for many people, especially outdoors, on small screens and for people with low vision. Good contrast also helps every visitor.

Is the color contrast checker free?

Yes. Check as many color pairs as you like, with no sign-up.

What is a good contrast ratio?

At least 4.5 to 1 for normal text, and 3 to 1 for large text, to meet WCAG level AA.

What colors can I enter?

HEX codes, with or without the # sign. Short codes like #fff also work.

How are the suggested colors chosen?

The tool keeps your text color's hue and makes it lighter or darker until it passes. It shows the smallest change that works.

Does this follow WCAG?

Yes. It uses the WCAG formula for relative luminance and the same ratio limits.
