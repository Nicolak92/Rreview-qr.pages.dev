How to make an email signature

  1. Fill in your details. Leave out anything you do not want.
  2. Pick a layout, a font and an accent color.
  3. Press Copy signature and paste it into your email settings. Or use Copy HTML code.

How to add it to your email

  • Gmail: Settings, See all settings, Signature. Paste the copied signature.
  • Outlook: Settings, Accounts, Signatures. Paste the copied signature.
  • Apple Mail: Settings, Signatures. Paste it, and turn off "Always match my default message font".

Tips for a good signature

Keep it short. Use one logo or photo that is already hosted on the web, because email programs often block images that are not. Use text for your contact details so it still works when images are off.

Is the email signature generator free?

Yes. Make as many signatures as you like, with no sign-up.

Why do I need an image web address?

Email programs show images from a web address. They often block images embedded in the signature itself. Upload your logo to your website or an image host and paste its address.

Will it look the same in every email program?

It uses simple tables and inline styles, which work in most programs. Small differences are normal.

Is my information stored?

No. The signature is built in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I edit it later?

Yes. Come back, change your details, and copy the new version.
