How to make a favicon

  1. Type a letter or emoji, or choose your logo.
  2. Pick two background colors, a text color and a shape.
  3. Check the preview at small sizes. Simple shapes look best.
  4. Download favicon.ico and the PNG sizes you need.
  5. Upload the files to your website, and paste the code into the head of your pages.

Which files do I need?

  • favicon.ico: works in every browser. Contains 16, 32 and 48 px sizes.
  • Apple icon (180 px): used when someone adds your site to an iPhone home screen.
  • 192 and 512 px PNG: used by Android and web apps.

Design tips

A favicon is tiny. Use one or two characters, strong contrast and no fine detail. A simple logo mark works better than a full logo with text.

Is the favicon generator free?

Yes. Make and download as many icons as you like, with no sign-up.

Is my logo uploaded?

No. The icon is made in your browser. Your image is never sent anywhere.

What size should a favicon be?

The .ico file holds 16, 32 and 48 px. Google asks for an icon that is a multiple of 48 px, so a 192 px PNG is also useful.

Can I use an emoji?

Yes. Type an emoji in the box. It is drawn with your device's emoji font.

How do I add the favicon to WordPress?

Upload the 512 px PNG as the Site Icon under Appearance, Customize, Site Identity.
