What is a Google review link?

A Google review link is a direct web address that opens the "write a review" box for your business. Customers do not have to search for you, find your listing and look for the review button. They tap the link and write.

How to create your Google review link

  1. Open your business in Google Maps and tap Share, then copy the link.
  2. Paste it in the box above and press Create review link.
  3. Copy the link, or copy one of the ready-made messages.
  4. Press Test link to check it opens the review form for the right business.

Where to use your review link

  • In text messages and WhatsApp after a sale or a visit
  • In follow-up emails and email signatures
  • On your website, invoices, receipts and thank-you pages
  • Turned into a QR code for your counter, menu or packaging

Follow Google's review rules

Do not offer discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews, and do not ask only your happiest customers. Ask everyone and let people write what they really think. Reviews that break Google's rules can be removed.

Is the Google review link generator free?

Yes. Create as many links as you like, with no sign-up.

Does it work with short maps.app.goo.gl links?

Yes. Paste the short link, a full Google Maps link, or a Place ID that starts with ChIJ.

How do I know the link opens the right business?

Press Test link before you share it. If it opens the wrong business, paste the full link from your browser's address bar instead of the short link.

Do I need a Google Business Profile?

Yes. Customers can only review a business that has a listing on Google Maps.

Does the link expire?

No. It keeps working as long as your Google Business Profile exists.

Can I turn the link into a QR code?

Yes. Use the Make a QR code button after you create the link, or make a printable sign with your logo.
