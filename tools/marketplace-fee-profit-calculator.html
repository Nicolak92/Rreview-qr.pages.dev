Fees

The examples are only starting points. Fees change often and differ by country, category, seller plan and payment method. Check your marketplace's current fee page and edit the numbers.

How to calculate your profit after marketplace fees

  1. Enter your selling price, shipping, and what the item costs you.
  2. Enter your marketplace and payment fees, or start from an example and edit it.
  3. Read your profit per sale, margin and markup.
  4. Check the break-even price and the price needed for your target margin.

What fees to include

  • Marketplace or transaction fee (a percentage)
  • Payment processing fee (a percentage plus a fixed amount)
  • Listing fees and advertising
  • Shipping, packaging and returns

Margin and markup are different

Margin is profit divided by the money you receive. Markup is profit divided by your cost. A 50% markup is only a 33% margin.

Is the marketplace fee calculator free?

Yes. Use it as often as you like, with no sign-up.

Does it work for Etsy, eBay, Amazon and Shopify?

Yes. It works for any marketplace. Enter the fee rates for the one you use, or start from an example and edit it.

Are the example fees up to date?

They are only examples. Marketplace fees change, so always check the current rates on the marketplace's own fee page.

What is the break-even price?

It is the lowest selling price at which you make zero profit after all fees and costs.

How is the target price worked out?

The tool finds the price that gives your chosen profit margin after fees, shipping and costs.
