How to pick a good podcast name

  1. Type your topic in one to three words.
  2. Choose a style: catchy, professional, funny or short.
  3. Press Generate names until something sparks. Star the ones you like.
  4. Search each favorite to check that the name is not already taken.

What makes a good podcast name?

  • Easy to say and to spell
  • Tells people what the show is about
  • Short enough to read in a podcast app
  • Not too close to a well-known show

Before you launch

Check the name on podcast apps, social media and as a web address. Make sure the name does not copy someone else's trademark.

Is the podcast name generator free?

Yes. Generate as many names as you like, with no sign-up.

Does it use AI?

No. It combines your topic with proven name patterns, so there is no AI cost and nothing is stored.

Are the names available?

The tool cannot check availability. Use the search link beside each name, and check podcast apps and social media.

How long should a podcast name be?

Two to four words works well. Shorter names are easier to remember and fit better in apps.

Can I use the names commercially?

The patterns are generic, but you are responsible for checking that a name does not infringe someone else's trademark.
