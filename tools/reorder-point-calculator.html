Order quantity (EOQ), optional

How to calculate your reorder point

  1. Enter your average daily demand and your supplier lead time.
  2. Choose a safety stock method. Simple uses your worst case. Statistical uses a service level.
  3. Read the safety stock and the reorder point.
  4. Add your order cost and unit cost to see the best order quantity.

The formulas

  • Reorder point = daily demand x lead time + safety stock
  • Simple safety stock = highest daily demand x longest lead time - average daily demand x average lead time
  • Statistical safety stock = Z x square root of (lead time x demand variance + demand squared x lead time variance)
  • EOQ = square root of (2 x annual demand x order cost / holding cost per unit)

When to use which method

Use the simple method if you have little data. Use the statistical method when you know how much your demand and lead time vary, and you want to choose a service level such as 95%.

Is the reorder point calculator free?

Yes. Use it as often as you like, with no sign-up.

What is a reorder point?

It is the stock level at which you should place a new order, so that new stock arrives before you run out.

What is safety stock?

Extra stock you keep to cover higher demand or late deliveries.

What service level should I choose?

95% is a common starting point. A higher level means fewer stockouts but more stock to hold.

What is EOQ?

Economic order quantity is the order size that gives the lowest total of ordering and holding costs. It assumes steady demand.
