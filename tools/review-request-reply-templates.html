Need your review link? Create it here.

How to use these templates

  1. Choose Ask for a review or Reply to a review.
  2. Add your business name. Add your review link or your contact details.
  3. Pick the channel or the star rating, and a tone.
  4. Press Another version until it sounds like you, then copy it.

Tips for asking for reviews

  • Ask right after a good experience, while it is fresh.
  • Keep it short and make the link one tap away.
  • Ask every customer, not only the happy ones.
  • Never offer rewards for reviews. Google can remove them.

Tips for replying to reviews

Reply to good and bad reviews. Stay polite, thank the person, and keep it short. For a bad review, apologize and invite them to contact you privately. Never argue in public.

Are these templates free?

Yes. Use them as often as you like, with no sign-up.

Is this AI?

No. The messages come from ready-written templates, so there is no AI cost and nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I edit the message?

Yes. Click in the box and change anything before you copy it.

Should I reply to every review?

Replying to most reviews shows customers that you care. Always reply to complaints.

Where do I get my review link?

Use the free Google Review Link Generator on this site. Paste your Google Maps link and copy the result.
