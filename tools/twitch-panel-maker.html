Text

Twitch panels are 320 pixels wide. Choose the 2x size for sharper images on large screens.

How to make Twitch panels

  1. Write one panel per line. Use a bar to add small text, like About Me | Who I am.
  2. Pick a design, a height and a border style. Add an emoji icon if you like.
  3. Download each panel, or press Download all.
  4. On Twitch, open your channel, turn on Edit Panels, add an image panel and upload the file.

Which panels should a channel have?

  • About me and what you stream
  • Schedule
  • Chat rules
  • Social links and Discord
  • Donations, subs and support

Design tips

Keep the same colors and fonts on every panel so your channel looks tidy. Use short titles, because panels are small.

Is the Twitch panel maker free?

Yes. Make and download as many panels as you like, with no sign-up.

What size is a Twitch panel?

Twitch panels are 320 pixels wide. The height is up to you. This tool offers common heights and a 2x size for sharper images.

Can I add links?

Add the link on Twitch when you upload the panel. In Twitch's panel editor, you can set a link for each image panel.

Are my panels uploaded anywhere?

No. They are made in your browser and never leave your device.

Can I use my own colors?

Yes. Choose Custom in the design list and pick your own colors.
