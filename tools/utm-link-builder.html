Your links

How to build a UTM link

  1. Enter the page you want people to visit.
  2. Name your campaign, for example spring_sale.
  3. Choose the channels where you will share the link. Each one gets its own link.
  4. Copy the links and use them in your posts, emails and ads.

What do the UTM parts mean?

  • utm_source: where the visit comes from, such as facebook or newsletter.
  • utm_medium: the type of channel, such as social, email or cpc.
  • utm_campaign: the name of the campaign or promotion.
  • utm_term and utm_content: optional. Used for paid keywords and to compare ads.

Good habits

Use lowercase and the same names every time, so your reports stay clean. Do not use UTM links for links inside your own website, because they can break your analytics sessions.

Is the UTM link builder free?

Yes. Build as many links as you like, with no sign-up.

Do I need Google Analytics?

UTM tags are read by Google Analytics and most other analytics tools. The tags only work if your site uses one of them.

Can I build links for several channels at once?

Yes. Choose several channels and you get one link for each.

Why use lowercase?

Analytics tools treat Facebook and facebook as two different sources. Lowercase keeps your data in one place.

Does my link keep its existing parameters?

Yes. Existing query parameters and anchors in your address are kept.
