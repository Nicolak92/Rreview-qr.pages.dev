Carriers choose their own divisor and rounding rules. Check your carrier's current terms. A divisor of 5000 cm³ per kg is about 139 in³ per lb, and 6000 is about 166.

How to calculate volumetric weight

  1. Enter the box length, width and height.
  2. Enter the actual weight and the number of boxes.
  3. See the volumetric weight for the common divisors 4000, 5000 and 6000.
  4. The chargeable weight is the higher of the actual weight and the volumetric weight.

The formula

Volumetric weight (kg) = length x width x height (cm) / divisor. Air and express carriers charge for whichever is greater: the real weight or the volumetric weight.

How to lower shipping cost

  • Use the smallest box that protects the product
  • Remove empty space and extra packaging
  • Ship light but bulky items by the cheapest service for volume

What is volumetric weight?

It is a weight worked out from the size of a package. Carriers use it so that large, light parcels are charged fairly for the space they take.

Which divisor should I use?

Use the one your carrier states. Common values are 5000 for many express services and 6000 for air freight. Check the current terms.

What is chargeable weight?

It is the higher of the actual weight and the volumetric weight. It is the weight your freight price is based on.
